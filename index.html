Frequently Asked Questions

What models are supported?
We support all major models including OpenAI's GPT-4o & GPT-4o Mini, Anthropic's Claude 3 Opus & 3.5 Sonnet, Google's Gemini 1.5 Pro, Meta's Llama 3, and Mistral Large.
How does Smart Router work?
Our Smart Router analyzes your prompt's intent, complexity, and required capabilities. It then matches it with the most efficient model that can handle the task, balancing cost and performance.
Can I use my own API keys?
Yes, Team plan users can integrate their own API keys for OpenAI, Anthropic, and other providers to use their existing balances and limits.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We do not use your conversational data to train our models, and all interactions are encrypted in transit and at rest.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, UPI, and net banking via our secure payment gateway.
