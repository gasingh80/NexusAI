Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bring my own API keys?
Yes! You can use your own API keys in Settings. When using your own keys, you only pay for the Kombat AI platform fee, and model usage is billed directly to your provider accounts.
What happens if I hit the message limit?
On the Free plan, you'll be paused until the next day. Pro and Team plans have soft limits to prevent abuse but are generally unlimited for normal human usage.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Your access will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle.
